# מול העדשה — Giglo identity

The approved mark combines an open lens ring, three prompt-text strokes and a coral recording dot. Its rounded geometry connects the camera/teleprompter product to Giglo's lime, ivory and coral visual language within the existing dark studio interface.

- iOS icon: `App/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`, 1024 × 1024 RGB PNG, opaque, square corners. The operating system applies its icon mask.
- In-app mark: `App/Assets.xcassets/LensLogo.imageset/LensLogo.png`, identical pixels to the app icon.
- The library header renders the Hebrew product name and the small Giglo endorsement as native, accessible text. The decorative bitmap is hidden from VoiceOver.
- Giglo endorsement colors: lime `#C3EE2B`, coral `#FF4F35`; app background `#171916`, ivory text `#F2F1E8`.

The icon is polished raster artwork derived from the approved visual concept; no editable vector master is included. Keep the two catalog images identical when replacing the symbol.
